<h2><a href="https://leetcode.com/problems/maximum-product-of-two-elements-in-an-array">1464. Maximum Product of Two Elements in an Array</a></h2>

<p>You are given an array of integers <code>nums</code>.</p>

<p>Choose two <strong>different</strong> indices <code>i</code> and <code>j</code> of that array.</p>

<p>Return the <strong>maximum</strong> value of <code>(nums[i] - 1) * (nums[j] - 1)</code>.</p>

<p>&nbsp;</p>
<p><span class="float-right -my-0.5 ml-2 inline-flex select-none items-center" data-example-lang-controls=""><span role="group" class="inline-flex h-6 items-center rounded-sd-md bg-sd-muted p-0.5" data-example-lang-toggle=""><button type="button" aria-pressed="true" class="h-5 max-w-[10rem] truncate whitespace-nowrap rounded-sd-sm border px-2 text-xs font-normal leading-none border-sd-border bg-sd-background text-sd-foreground" fdprocessedid="ma5vc">Original</button><button type="button" aria-pressed="false" class="h-5 max-w-[10rem] truncate whitespace-nowrap rounded-sd-sm border px-2 text-xs font-normal leading-none border-transparent text-sd-muted-foreground hover:text-sd-foreground" fdprocessedid="7gdrn">Java</button></span></span><strong class="example">Example 1:</strong></p>

<pre><strong>Input:</strong> nums = [3,4,5,2]
<strong>Output:</strong> 12 
<strong>Explanation:</strong> If you choose the indices i=1 and j=2 (indexed from 0), you will get the maximum value, that is, (nums[1]-1)*(nums[2]-1) = (4-1)*(5-1) = 3*4 = 12. 
</pre>

<p><strong class="example">Example 2:</strong></p>

<pre><strong>Input:</strong> nums = [1,5,4,5]
<strong>Output:</strong> 16
<strong>Explanation:</strong> Choosing the indices i=1 and j=3 (indexed from 0), you will get the maximum value of (5-1)*(5-1) = 16.
</pre>

<p><strong class="example">Example 3:</strong></p>

<pre><strong>Input:</strong> nums = [3,7]
<strong>Output:</strong> 12
</pre>

<p><strong>Constraints:</strong></p>

<ul>
	<li><code>2 &lt;= nums.length &lt;= 500</code></li>
	<li><code>1 &lt;= nums[i] &lt;= 10^3</code></li>
</ul>
